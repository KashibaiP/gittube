GitTube

Repositories worth a look on GitHub, grouped into lanes by language and topic. Every card links to the real repository.

Data comes from the GitHub repository search API. scripts/update.py rewrites data.js once a day and keeps a snapshot of each repo's star count. The "today" gain compares the latest snapshot with the previous day's, and "this week" with one from a week earlier, so they appear after the second daily refresh and after the eighth.

Lanes you add yourself are searched live on GitHub from your browser and saved only in this browser (no account, nothing sent to us). GitHub allows about 10 anonymous searches a minute, so a rate-limit message just means wait a bit.

Weekly digest: feed.xml is an RSS feed with one entry per week (the fastest risers and the top of each lane). Add its address to any RSS reader, or to a free service that turns RSS into email.

Stars and forks are counts at the time of the last refresh. Repos that mention certain adult-content words are left out.
